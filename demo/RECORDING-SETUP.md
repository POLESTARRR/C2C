# Recording on a MacBook Air, with your voice

Do this once before you record. Ten minutes.

## 1. Turn off interruptions

* Click the **Control Centre** icon in the top right menu bar
* Click **Focus**, then **Do Not Disturb**, and set it to **On**
* Quit WhatsApp, Slack, Mail, Messages. A notification banner in the middle of
  your demo is the single most common way a take gets ruined.

## 2. Clean up the screen

* Put the browser in fullscreen
* Hide the bookmarks bar with **Cmd + Shift + B**
* Close every tab except `localhost:9000`
* Set browser zoom to about 110% with **Cmd and the plus key**, twice. Text needs
  to survive being compressed by YouTube.

## 3. Start the screen recorder with your microphone on

This is the step people get wrong. Screen recording does NOT capture your voice
unless you switch the microphone on first.

1. Press **Cmd + Shift + 5**. A small toolbar appears at the bottom of the screen.
2. Click **Options** in that toolbar.
3. Under **Microphone**, click **MacBook Air Microphone**. It must show a tick
   next to it. If it says None, your video will be silent.
4. Under **Save to**, choose **Desktop** so you can find the file afterwards.
5. Back on the toolbar, click **Record Entire Screen** (the leftmost icon).
6. Click **Record**.

To stop, click the small **stop** square in the top right menu bar, or press
**Cmd + Ctrl + Esc**.

## 4. Test fifteen seconds first. Do not skip this.

Record fifteen seconds of yourself talking. Stop. Open the file from your
Desktop and play it back.

Check:
* Can you hear your voice clearly?
* Is there loud fan noise or echo?
* Is the text on screen readable?

If your voice is quiet, move closer to the laptop. The microphone is next to the
keyboard, so speak toward the screen, not away from it. If there is echo, move to
a room with soft furnishing. Bad audio ruins a demo faster than a bad interface.

## 5. Have these ready before you press record

* This laptop: the browser on `localhost:9000`, and a second window with
  `backend/swiggy_mcp_client.py` open in your editor
* Your phone: `demo/SPOKEN-SCRIPT.md` open, so you can read it while recording
* Clipboard or a text file: the vada pav URL and the Hindi transcript

## 6. While recording

* Read from your phone. Do not read from the laptop screen, the camera sees it.
* Speak a little slower than feels natural. Everyone rushes on their first take.
* If you fumble a word, do not stop. Pause, breathe, say the sentence again and
  keep going. You will cut it out later.
* Record the whole thing three times. Pick the best one afterwards.
