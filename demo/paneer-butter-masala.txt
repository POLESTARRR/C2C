Now the spices. One teaspoon of red chilli powder, half a teaspoon of turmeric powder, one teaspoon of coriander powder, and one teaspoon of garam masala. And of course salt to taste.

For the fat, we're using two tablespoons of butter and one tablespoon of oil. Butter is what makes this dish, so don't skip it.

At the end we'll add 50 ml of fresh cream and one tablespoon of kasuri methi, crushed between your palms. That kasuri methi is non negotiable, it's what makes it taste like the restaurant version.

Oh and a teaspoon of sugar to balance the tomatoes. Trust me on this one.
